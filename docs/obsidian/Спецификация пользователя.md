# CATAN — Game Rules Specification

## 1. Base Game

### Goal

Players compete to reach the victory-point target defined by the selected scenario.

For the standard base game, the target is 10 Victory Points.

Victory Points are primarily gained from:

- Settlements
- Cities
- Longest Road
- Largest Army
- Victory Point development cards

The first player who satisfies the scenario's victory condition during their turn wins.

---

## 2. Resources

The five standard resources are:

- Lumber
- Brick
- Wool
- Grain
- Ore

Terrain produces:

Forest → Lumber  
Hills → Brick  
Pasture → Wool  
Fields → Grain  
Mountains → Ore

The desert normally produces nothing.

---

## 3. Initial Setup

Players choose their colors.

Each player begins with:

- 5 settlements
- 4 cities
- 15 roads

Initial placement occurs in snake order.

First round:

Player 1 → Player 2 → Player 3 → Player 4

Second round:

Player 4 → Player 3 → Player 2 → Player 1

During each placement, the player places:

1 settlement  
1 connected road

After placing the second settlement, the player receives one resource from each producing land hex adjacent to that settlement.

The normal settlement distance rule applies during setup.

---

## 4. Turn Structure

A normal turn consists of:

### Phase A — Roll Dice

The active player rolls two six-sided dice.

If the result is not 7, all terrain hexes with that number produce resources.

A settlement receives 1 resource from each producing adjacent hex.

A city receives 2 resources.

Resources are limited by the bank's available supply.

### Phase B — Resolve 7

If a 7 is rolled:

Players holding more than 7 resource cards must discard half of them, rounded down.

After discards are complete, the active player moves the robber.

The robber must move to another valid land hex.

The robber blocks resource production from that hex.

If another player has a settlement or city adjacent to the robber's new location and has at least one resource card, the active player may steal one random resource card from an eligible player.

### Phase C — Trade and Build

The active player may trade and build in any valid order while resources and legal actions are available.

### Phase D — End Turn

The active player ends their turn and play passes to the next player.

---

## 5. Building Costs

### Road

Cost:

1 Lumber  
1 Brick

A road must connect legally to the player's existing network.

### Settlement

Cost:

1 Lumber  
1 Brick  
1 Wool  
1 Grain

A settlement must:

- be placed on a valid board intersection;
- connect to one of the player's roads, except during initial setup;
- obey the distance rule.

### City

Cost:

3 Ore  
2 Grain

A city may only replace one of the player's own settlements.

The settlement piece returns to the player's supply.

### Development Card

Cost:

1 Ore  
1 Wool  
1 Grain

The player draws the top card of the development deck.

---

## 6. Distance Rule

A settlement may only be built if the three neighboring intersections contain no settlement or city.

This is equivalent to requiring at least two road edges between neighboring settlements/cities.

---

## 7. Trading

### Domestic Trade

During their own turn, a player may offer resource trades to other players.

Only the active player may initiate a trade.

Players may accept, reject, or negotiate.

Players may not give resources away for nothing.

### Maritime Trade

Without a harbor:

4 identical resources → 1 resource of choice

Generic harbor:

3 identical resources → 1 resource of choice

Specialized 2:1 harbor:

2 resources of the indicated type → 1 resource of choice

A harbor becomes usable when the player owns a settlement or city on its harbor intersection.

---

## 8. Development Cards

### Knight

Move the robber and steal one random resource card from an eligible player.

A played Knight contributes toward Largest Army.

### Road Building

Build two roads without paying their normal resource costs.

In Seafarers scenarios, scenario/rule configuration determines whether eligible ship placement may also be used where appropriate.

### Year of Plenty

Take any two available resource cards from the bank.

The bank may never contain a negative quantity.

### Monopoly

Choose one resource type.

Every other player gives the active player all resource cards of that type.

### Victory Point

Provides a hidden Victory Point.

Victory Point cards are revealed when required to establish victory.

### Development Card Restrictions

A newly purchased development card normally cannot be played on the same turn it was purchased.

Normally only one development card may be played during a turn, excluding Victory Point revelation where applicable.

A failed or invalid development-card command must not consume the card or mutate game state.

---

## 9. Longest Road

A player must have a continuous legal road network of at least 5 segments to qualify.

Longest Road is worth 2 Victory Points.

Another player's settlement or city can interrupt a route.

Ties must follow the configured official rule rather than automatically removing the award.

---

## 10. Largest Army

A player qualifies after playing at least 3 Knight cards.

Largest Army is worth 2 Victory Points.

Another player takes the award only by exceeding the current holder's played-Knight count.

---

# Seafarers

Seafarers extends the base game.

Unless a Seafarers rule or scenario rule overrides it, Base Game rules continue to apply.

Different Seafarers scenarios may use different map layouts, victory targets, exploration systems, and special objectives.

---

## 11. Ships

Ships are used to extend a player's network across sea edges.

Ship cost:

1 Lumber  
1 Wool

A new ship must connect legally to:

- one of the player's coastal settlements or cities; or
- the end of an existing valid shipping route.

Ships occupy sea edges in a similar way to how roads occupy land edges.

A ship may not be placed on an invalid or occupied edge.

---

## 12. Roads and Ships

Roads form routes across land.

Ships form routes across water.

A road and ship route normally connect through the player's own coastal settlement or city.

Road/ship continuity must be calculated according to the selected scenario's official Seafarers rules.

---

## 13. Moving Ships

Unlike roads, certain ships may be moved.

Only an eligible open-end ship may be relocated.

A ship that forms part of a closed route may not be moved.

A shipping route becomes closed when its relevant endpoints are anchored by the player's settlements or cities.

Ship movement must preserve all route invariants.

Only legal ships may be selected for movement.

---

## 14. Settling New Islands

Ships may connect the player's network to new islands.

When a player reaches a valid land intersection, they may build a settlement there if all normal settlement conditions are satisfied.

Some scenarios award additional Victory Points for establishing the player's first settlement on a new island or for other exploration objectives.

These bonuses belong to scenario configuration, not to universal Base Game logic.

---

## 15. Gold Fields

Gold fields use number tokens and produce when their number is rolled.

Instead of receiving a fixed resource type, an eligible player chooses a standard resource.

A settlement normally provides one chosen resource.

A city normally provides two resource selections, subject to the scenario's rules and bank availability.

Gold itself is not a sixth resource card.

---

## 16. Pirate

Some Seafarers scenarios use a pirate.

The pirate occupies a sea hex.

While present, it interferes with nearby shipping according to the scenario's rules.

When the rules require the player to move a robber-type piece, applicable scenarios may allow or require interaction with the pirate.

Robber and pirate behavior must be implemented separately rather than treating the pirate as a cosmetic robber.

---

## 17. Longest Trade Route

When Seafarers rules apply, ships participate in route calculations according to the scenario rules.

Road and ship networks may interact through valid coastal settlements/cities.

Foreign settlements can interrupt routes.

Open and closed shipping routes must be handled correctly.

The implementation must not simply count every connected road and ship without evaluating route topology.

---

## 18. Exploration and Fog

Some scenarios contain unexplored or hidden hexes.

When a ship reaches an exploration boundary, the scenario may reveal:

- Sea
- Standard terrain
- Gold fields
- Other scenario-specific tiles or tokens

Exploration behavior must be implemented as scenario logic rather than a universal Seafarers action.

---

## 19. Scenario Rules

A Seafarers scenario may define:

- Map layout
- Number-token distribution
- Starting islands
- Hidden/fog tiles
- Pirate usage
- Special Victory Points
- Victory target
- Special tokens
- Exploration rewards
- Additional objectives

The engine must therefore separate:

Base rules

from

Seafarers mechanics

from

Scenario-specific rules.

---

# Engine Invariants

The server/game engine is authoritative.

Clients request actions; they do not dictate their results.

Clients may never directly grant resources.

Clients may never choose dice results outside an explicitly enabled debug/test environment.

Coordinates must exist on the current map before a piece can be placed or moved.

The bank may never contain negative resources.

An invalid command must leave game state unchanged.

Hidden information must not be included in snapshots sent to players who are not allowed to see it.

Every action must validate:

- current game phase;
- active player where applicable;
- ownership;
- coordinates;
- resource cost;
- piece availability;
- board topology;
- scenario restrictions;

before mutating game state.

- Сервер выбирает starter по policy Random (default) или Host. Snake поворачивается от него; первый main turn у того же игрока. Pid/P1/цвет не определяют старт, rematch выбирает снова.
- Dice Random (default) сохраняет две secure independent d6. Optional Balanced balanced_v1: secure shuffled bag из 36 ordered outcomes, без replacement; при 12 оставшихся следующий draw заменяет bag новым полным. Веса полного bag соответствуют 2d6; фиксированное количество любого числа/ресурса в 36 последовательных draws не гарантируется. Private bag не раскрывается, invalid command не расходует его.
- Turn timer Off (default), 30/60/90/120s. Server main-turn expiry до Roll делает обычный Roll +20s grace; после Roll без обязательного состояния — End. Pending discard/robber/pirate/victim/gold/free-road и будущие server pending требуют explicit resolution, случайных choices нет. Setup не автоматизируется. Неотправленная локальная форма не является server pending.
- Bank counts Visible (casual default) или Hidden (прежняя availability-only privacy). Visible намеренно допускает deductions в двух игроках. Deck/count/order и future cards остаются скрытыми: public deck-count policy не принималась.
- Target VP начинается с выбранного preset RulesConfig; явный host override 3..30 сохраняется при выборе карты/rematch. Карты и другие gameplay rules не меняются. Настройки меняются только host в lobby и locked после Start.
- Unique red/blue/orange/white/green/purple являются presentation, не identity. Цвет сохраняется при reconnect/compact rematch. Room/game chat server-owned, plain text, bounded recent history; это коммуникация вне engine log, не действие GameState.
